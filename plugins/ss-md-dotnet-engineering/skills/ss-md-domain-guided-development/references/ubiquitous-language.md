## Use a ubiquitous language

Use these guidelines when naming or interpreting domain elements. Establish the bounded context before making terminology consistent or merging similar concepts.

- Use the team's agreed domain terms consistently within each bounded context, including types, operations, tests, and discussions.
- Name domain behavior by its meaning; retain technical vocabulary for technical mechanisms rather than disguising them as business concepts.
- Resolve ambiguity within a context; preserve legitimate differences across contexts. Verify that a rename preserves the intended meaning and affected contracts, not just naming consistency.
- When understanding changes, update misleading names and affected uses together. Preserve compatibility at public boundaries through an explicit migration, not an unexplained breaking rename.
